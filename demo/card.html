Card

A padded white surface with a soft shadow and generous radius. Every sub-part — header, body, footer, media — is optional and combined in markup.

Anatomy

Card title

This body text, the white background, the shadow, and the rounded corners all come from the .card component class defined in css/components/card.css.

Minimal

.card on its own is a valid card — no sub-part is required.

Media and layout variants

The image rules are descendant selectors — the <img> itself takes no class. .card-media cancels .card's padding with negative margins, so it must be a direct child of the card.

Classes

.card
The surface: white background, shadow-ds-md, rounded-ds-lg, p-5.
.card-header
Serif title with bottom margin. Use on whatever heading level the page needs.
.card-body
Relaxed leading, neutral body color.
.card-footer
Top border plus spacing above it. Combine with layout utilities for the row itself.
.card-media
Wraps an <img> and bleeds it to the top and side edges. Direct child of .card only.
.card-horizontal
Layout modifier — combine with .card for image-beside-content.
.card-horizontal-media
Fixed 96×96 thumbnail slot for .card-horizontal.
.card-book-cover
Portrait 2:3 cover slot, one third of the card width, top-aligned.
.card-book-author
Muted byline line, sized to sit under .card-header.
