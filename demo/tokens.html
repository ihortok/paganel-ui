Design tokens

Everything below is a @theme variable in css/tokens.css, which means Tailwind generates real utilities from it — bg-brand-600, rounded-ds-lg, shadow-ds-md — and your own markup can use them directly alongside the component classes.

Brand

A warm terracotta/clay ramp. Only these five steps are defined — the lightness progression is deliberate, so text contrast against white and black is known rather than guessed.

Neutrals

Warm "stone" neutrals — a small positive chroma at a warm hue rather than pure achromatic gray, subtle enough not to read as tinted.

neutral-50

oklch(0.98 0.004 75) — the page background

neutral-200

oklch(0.90 0.007 75) — every border

neutral-700

oklch(0.35 0.010 70) — body text

neutral-900

oklch(0.16 0.008 65) — headings

Only four neutral steps are overridden

Anything else — neutral-400, neutral-500, which the form hints, book bylines and sidebar section titles all use — falls through to Tailwind's stock cool neutral, sitting next to these warm ones. Worth knowing before you reach for a step that isn't in the list above.

neutral-400 — not defined by this package; Tailwind's default.

Shadow

Warm-tinted rather than pure black, and layered — a soft "lifted paper" feel instead of a flat drop shadow.

shadow-ds-sm
shadow-ds-md — used by .card

Type

--font-serif overrides Tailwind's default to prioritize a warmer, more literary system serif where one exists, falling back cleanly elsewhere. Zero dependency — no font files ship. Headings and .card-header use it; body text stays on the sans stack.
